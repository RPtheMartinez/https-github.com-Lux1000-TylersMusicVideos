# Tyler, The Creator - Music Videos

A web page showcasing Tyler, The Creator's music videos with an elegant album-based layout.

## Features

- 📀 **Album Display**: Album covers displayed on the left, tracklists on the right
- 🎵 **Clickable Songs**: Songs with YouTube music videos appear as hyperlinks
- 🎬 **Modal Video Player**: Videos open in a foreground modal window
- 📱 **Responsive Design**: Works seamlessly on desktop, tablet, and mobile
- ⌨️ **Keyboard Support**: Close modal with Escape key

## How to Use

1. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser
2. Browse through Tyler, The Creator's albums
3. Click on any blue hyperlinked song title to watch the music video
4. Close the video player by:
   - Clicking the × button
   - Clicking outside the video
   - Pressing the Escape key

## Albums Included

- **Flower Boy (2017)** - 14 tracks
- **IGOR (2019)** - 12 tracks with 2 music videos
- **Call Me If You Get Lost (2021)** - 14 tracks with 2 music videos
- **Goblin (2011)** - 15 tracks with 1 music video
- **Cherry Bomb (2015)** - 13 tracks with 2 music videos

## Technologies

- HTML5
- CSS3 (with responsive design and animations)
- Vanilla JavaScript (no frameworks required)
- YouTube Embedded Player API
